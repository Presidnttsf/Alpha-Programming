Practice Rule
First understand the problem.
Write the logic in your own words.
Try to solve it without looking at the solution.
Test with different inputs.
Check edge cases such as 0, negative numbers, and duplicate values.
Only look at a solution after making your own attempt.
After solving a problem, try to solve it again using a different approach.
This gives you 155 progressively arranged C problems, from basic variables and conditions all the way to pointers, structures, dynamic memory, file handling, and projects.
